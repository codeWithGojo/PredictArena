About PredictArena

PredictArena is a pure frontend sports analysis & prediction tool built for football (Europe Top 5), NBA and African CODM esports.

All calculations run in your browser. No accounts, no tracking. Predictions are statistical models based on form and strength ratings — not guaranteed outcomes.

Data sources

  • Football & Basketball match data: SportScore (free public API)
  • CODM Africa: curated from recent tournaments (Carry1st Africa Cup, Kon10dr, regional events)

Built as a learning project. Predictions are for entertainment and analysis only.
